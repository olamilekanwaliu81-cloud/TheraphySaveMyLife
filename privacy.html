This Privacy Policy explains how Paula Axelrod ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit therapysavedmylife.com (the "Site") and when you sign up to receive the free guide or email updates. By using the Site, you agree to this Policy.

1. What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us. When you fill in the free-guide form, we collect your:

  • First name
  • Email address

We do not ask for or collect payment details, home addresses, or other sensitive information on this Site. Any purchase of the book happens on Amazon, under Amazon's own privacy policy — not here.

2. How we use your information

We use your first name and email address to:

  • Send you the free guide, "How to Find the Right Therapist."
  • Send occasional updates, encouragement, and news from Paula about the book and related topics.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

3. Email service (Brevo)

We use Brevo (a third-party email marketing platform) to store our contact list and send emails. When you sign up, the information you enter is securely sent to and stored by Brevo on our behalf. Brevo processes this data according to its own privacy policy and applicable data-protection laws.

4. Links to Amazon and other sites

The Site links to the book on Amazon and may link to other outside websites (for example, therapist directories mentioned in the free guide). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites. Please review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

5. Cookies and tracking

This Site itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Third-party tools we use (such as the embedded sign-up form and web fonts) may set limited technical cookies needed to work correctly. If you later add analytics or advertising tools (e.g. Google Analytics or the Meta pixel), update this section to describe them.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe anytime. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. One click removes you from our list.
  • Access, correct, or delete your data. Email us (see below) and we will help you view, update, or permanently delete the personal information we hold about you.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have additional rights over your personal data. We honor those rights.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep your information for as long as you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data, we remove it from our active list promptly.

8. Children's privacy

This Site and book deal with mature themes and are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us their information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact us at:
